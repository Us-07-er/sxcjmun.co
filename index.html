About SXCJ MUN

SXCJ Model United Nations (SXCJ MUN) is a dynamic platform that brings together young minds to engage in meaningful dialogue, debate, and diplomacy. Rooted in the spirit of global citizenship, the conference aims to simulate real-world international relations while fostering critical thinking, negotiation, and leadership.

Over two days, delegates will collaborate, challenge perspectives, and craft resolutions to some of the world’s most pressing issues — developing not just solutions, but the confidence and clarity to lead beyond the committee room.
