About SXCJ MUN

SXCJ Model United Nations is a platform where dialogue meets diplomacy. Delegates engage in global issues, develop negotiation skills, and learn leadership through simulation of international relations.

Over two days, participants debate, collaborate, and craft solutions — building confidence and perspective that extends beyond the committee room.
